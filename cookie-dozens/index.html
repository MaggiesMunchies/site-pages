The flavors

Every cookie is a 1/4 LB™ cookie, baked fresh in Manchester.

The Mix: two each of Chocolate Chip, Molasses, Oatmeal Raisin, Coffee Cake, Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip, and our seasonal flavor. The seasonal flavor is Pumpkin Maple-Pecan Crunch through November 30 and Cinnamon Sugar after December 1.

  • Chocolate Chip Our classic Chocolate Chip, loaded with chocolate chips in a rich, buttery cookie.
  • Coffee Cake Coffee Cake, warm cinnamon and brown sugar, like a slice of coffee cake in cookie form.
  • Molasses Molasses, deep and dark with old-fashioned spice in every bite.
  • Oatmeal Raisin Oatmeal Raisin, hearty oats and plump raisins in a soft, chewy cookie.
  • Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip Peanut Butter Chocolate Chip, peanut butter dough studded with chocolate chips.
  • Pumpkin Maple-Pecan Crunch Pumpkin Maple-Pecan Crunch, our seasonal flavor through November 30, with maple sweetness and a pecan crunch.
  • Cinnamon Sugar Cinnamon Sugar, rolled in cinnamon sugar. The seasonal flavor for orders filled after December 1.

Questions

Can I order just one dozen?

Yes, and welcome. One dozen or many, for an office, a holiday table, a birthday, or any occasion. There is no minimum.

How much notice do you need?

We ask for at minimum a week's notice. Short on time? Send the form anyway. We will do our best, but we cannot guarantee a date inside a week.

What do pickup and delivery cost?

Pickup at our Manchester bakery is free. Local delivery is a flat $20. Our driver drops the boxes at your door.

How does payment work?

We check your date and email an invoice. Payment is due on receipt, and paying it confirms your date.

Will you set up or serve at our event?

No. We drop off and go. The cookies arrive boxed and ready; we do not set up or serve at events.
